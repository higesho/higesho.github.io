Quests

92 of them, by the character level you need to take them on. Most are given out in the towns; some build a level of their own, and some wait for you deep in a dungeon. What is printed here is the text you are given when you accept. If you would rather not know what you will be asked, open this after taking the quest.

If you wish to show your allegience you must search the city and destroy the lair that these thieves call home. My troops report finding evidence of the thieves in the northeastern quarter.
