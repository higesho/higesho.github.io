Note Note

Weight 0.1 lb

Welcome to Sil. The game is set within a vast dungeon called Angband. You have journeyed far into the north to reach Angband, and at great cost have found your way in. You plan to descend 1,000 ft into the earth, to find the dark enemy Morgoth and to cut a Silmaril from his crown, so that its light might once again be free. As with many other Roguelike games, Sil's graphics are represented by text: you are represented by the @, the + is a door, the ~ is a note, the blocks are walls. Try moving around with the keypad or arrow keys to reach the note. (You may need to press Numlock if your keypad doesn't work).
